- `MOQOutputTransport` now holds the bot's first audio frame until the peer has subscribed to the bot's audio track (up to `MOQParams.audio_out_subscriber_timeout`, 15s by default), so a bot that greets the user on `client-ready` is audible on a cold start. Previously the audio was written to a track nobody was subscribed to yet and dropped, while the greeting's text still arrived via the replayed transcript stream. The `moq` extra now requires `moq-rs` 0.4.6, which exposes the subscriber signal.
